# Product & Customer Analysis Workbook

**Audience:** merchandising & CRM analytics  
**Question:** Which categories/products win, and how do loyalty segments drive LTV?

## Dashboards / sheets

### 1. Categories
- Monthly category revenue lines
- Category revenue share (pie)
- Units sold by category
- **Filters:** Date, Category

### 2. Customer LTV
- Avg lifetime revenue by loyalty tier
- AOV by loyalty tier
- LTV distribution histogram
- Top states by lifetime revenue
- **Filters:** Loyalty tier, State

### 3. Product Rankings
- Top 15 products by net revenue
- Top brands
- Units vs margin scatter (size = revenue)
- **Filters:** Category, Brand
