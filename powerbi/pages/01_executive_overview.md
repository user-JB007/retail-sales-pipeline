# Page 01 — Executive Overview

**Audience:** Hiring managers / retail ops leadership  
**Filters:** Date range, Region, Channel, Store type

## Visuals

1. **KPI cards (row):** Net Revenue · Transactions · Avg Order Value · Revenue MoM %
2. **Line/area:** Daily Net Revenue ($K) — `fact_daily_store_sales`
3. **Bar:** Net Revenue by Region
4. **Column:** Revenue by Category (from category mart)
5. **Card/multi-row:** Gross margin %, units sold, store-day count, latest month revenue

## Interactions

- Region slicer filters KPIs + daily trend.
- Click category bar → highlight related region contribution if both facts share date filter.
